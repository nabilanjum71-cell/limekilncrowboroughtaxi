Local Taxi Cover in Rotherfield

Limekiln Crowborough Taxis covers Rotherfield's hilltop High Street, from St Denys' Church and the King's Arms down through North Street. Rotherfield no longer has its own railway station, so most local journeys connect back to Crowborough or Tunbridge Wells for onward travel — which is exactly the run we do every day.

Getting Around Rotherfield

Rotherfield sits on a hilltop around St Denys' Church, with the High Street and North Street forming the heart of the village. Since the old Rotherfield and Mark Cross railway station closed in 1965, most residents travel via Crowborough station in Jarvis Brook or Tunbridge Wells for train connections — we run both routes regularly. The B2100 links Rotherfield to Crowborough and on to Wadhurst.
