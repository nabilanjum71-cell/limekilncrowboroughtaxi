Local Taxi Cover in Uckfield

Limekiln Crowborough Taxis runs regular fixed-fare journeys between Crowborough and Uckfield, covering the High Street, Uckfield Community Hospital, and Uckfield railway station near Bell Lane bus station. We take the A26, which is a straightforward, reliable run in either direction.

Getting Around Uckfield

Uckfield's High Street runs up towards the older part of town around Church Street, and we cover the full length of it for shop and restaurant pickups. For hospital appointments, Uckfield Community Hospital is less than a mile from the railway station and easy to reach quickly from the A26. If you're catching a train, the Bus Station and Rail Station sit close together near the town centre, and we can time a pickup to match your platform or your departure.
