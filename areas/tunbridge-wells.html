Local Taxi Cover in Tunbridge Wells

Limekiln Crowborough Taxis runs regular fixed-fare journeys between Crowborough and Tunbridge Wells, from town centre spots like the Pantiles and Royal Victoria Place through to Tunbridge Wells Hospital at Pembury and both train stations. We take the A26 via Frant, which keeps journey times consistent even when the town centre gets busy on market days or during Pantiles events.

Getting Around Tunbridge Wells

Tunbridge Wells is served by two stations — the mainline station in the town centre and High Brooms a little further out — both of which we cover for early departures and late arrivals. For hospital appointments, Tunbridge Wells Hospital sits on Tonbridge Road in Pembury, just off the A21; we build in extra time on outpatient runs in case a clinic overruns. If you're heading into town for the shops or a night out around the Pantiles or Calverley Road, tell us your nearest landmark and we'll confirm the exact pickup point at booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you pick up from Tunbridge Wells train station?+
Yes. We regularly collect from both Tunbridge Wells mainline station and High Brooms station, including early trains for Gatwick and Heathrow departures. Just give us your platform or the station forecourt as the pickup point when you book.
Can you take me to Tunbridge Wells Hospital at Pembury?+
Yes. We do regular transfers to Tunbridge Wells Hospital on Tonbridge Road, Pembury, for outpatient appointments and visiting. Fixed fare, confirmed before you travel, with flexibility if an appointment overruns.
How do you get from Crowborough to Tunbridge Wells?+
We run the A26 route via Frant, which normally takes 20–25 minutes depending on traffic through the town centre. If the Pantiles or Mount Ephraim area is busy we'll adjust the route to keep you on time.
Can I get an airport taxi from Tunbridge Wells?+
Yes. We offer fixed-price airport transfers from Tunbridge Wells to Gatwick, Heathrow, Stansted, Luton and London City airports, with all vehicle types available.
Do you cover pickups from the Pantiles and Royal Victoria Place?+
Yes, and anywhere else in the town centre. Tell us your nearest landmark — the Pantiles, Royal Victoria Place, Calverley Park or Dunorlan Park — and we'll confirm the exact pickup point when you book.
