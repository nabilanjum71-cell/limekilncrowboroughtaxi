About Limekiln Crowborough Taxis

Who We Are

Limekiln Crowborough Taxis is a locally based taxi service operating from Crowborough, East Sussex. We specialise in airport transfers to all five major London airports, as well as local journeys across Crowborough and the surrounding villages.

We were set up with a simple idea: passengers should know exactly what they are paying before they get in the car. Every fare is fixed, confirmed at booking and the same in both directions.

Why Passengers Choose Us

  • Fixed fares — confirmed on booking with no changes on the day
  • 4 vehicle options from Saloon to 8-seat MPV
  • Live flight tracking — delays adjusted automatically, no charge
  • Meet and greet inside the terminal on request
  • Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year
  • Licensed, insured and DBS-checked drivers
  • Local knowledge of Crowborough and all surrounding villages

Our Coverage Area

We cover Crowborough and all surrounding villages including Tunbridge Wells, Uckfield, Mayfield, Rotherfield, Jarvis Brook, Five Ashes, Frant, Wadhurst and Heathfield. Not sure if we cover your area? Just call us.
