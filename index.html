Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate your fares?+
All our fares are fixed and confirmed at the time of booking. The price you are quoted is the price you pay — no meters, no surge pricing, no hidden extras.
Do you charge extra for early morning or late night pickups?+
No. Our fares are the same 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including bank holidays. The price quoted covers any time of day.
What happens if my flight is delayed?+
We track every flight automatically. If your flight is delayed we simply adjust your pickup time at no extra charge. You don't need to call us — we monitor it ourselves.
How many passengers and bags can you take?+
Our Saloon takes up to 4 passengers and 4 standard cases. We also offer Estate, MPV 6 (up to 6 passengers) and MPV 8 (up to 8 passengers). Choose your vehicle when you get a quote.
How far in advance should I book?+
We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance for airport transfers. Same-day bookings are accepted subject to availability — call us directly for urgent bookings.
Do you cover areas outside Crowborough?+
Yes. We cover Crowborough and all surrounding villages including Tunbridge Wells, Uckfield, Mayfield, Rotherfield, Wadhurst, Frant, Heathfield and more.
Is the same price charged for the return journey?+
Yes. Our fixed fares apply in both directions — Crowborough to airport and airport back to Crowborough — always at the same confirmed price.
Do you offer a meet and greet service at the airport?+
Yes. On arrival transfers your driver will meet you inside the terminal with a name board. Just let us know when you book.
