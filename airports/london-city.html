About This Route

Limekiln Crowborough Taxis provides fixed-price transfers between Crowborough and London City Airport (LCY), covering Single terminal. The journey is approximately 42 miles and takes around 70–85 minutes depending on traffic.

We cover all villages in the Crowborough area including Tunbridge Wells, Uckfield, Mayfield, Rotherfield, Wadhurst and more — all at the same fixed fare as the Crowborough base price or marginally adjusted for your exact pickup point.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a taxi from Crowborough to London City Airport?+
A Saloon from Crowborough to London City Airport is £136. Larger vehicles are also available — Estate £156, MPV 6 £177, MPV 8 £211. All prices are fixed and confirmed at booking.
How long does it take to get to London City Airport from Crowborough?+
Allow approximately 70–85 minutes. We always build in extra buffer time on every booking.
Is the return journey the same price?+
Yes. The same fixed fare applies from Crowborough to the airport and from the airport back to Crowborough.
What if my flight is delayed?+
We track all flights automatically. If your flight is delayed we adjust your pickup at no extra charge.
